Section 01
Dashboard
Your business overview at a glance

The Dashboard is the first page you see after logging in. It shows you a quick summary of how your business is doing — how many orders came in, how much revenue was made, and recent activity.

📌 What you see on the Dashboard

Pending Bookings

Service bookings that customers made but you haven't approved yet.

Pending Orders

Shop orders placed but not yet confirmed by you.

Total Bookings

All bookings in the selected time period.

Total Orders

All shop orders in the selected time period.

Products Live

How many products are currently visible on your shop.

Paid Revenue

Total money received from paid orders + paid bookings (includes shipping).

🔍 Filtering by Period (All Time / This Month)

At the top of the dashboard there is a filter bar. You can switch between:

  • All Time — shows totals since the website launched
  • This Month — shows only the current month's numbers
  • Custom Month/Year — pick any month and year from the dropdowns
Example

You want to see how much revenue came in during June 2026. Select "June" and "2026" from the dropdowns. All the cards and charts will update to show June-only data.

📥 Downloading Reports

  • Download Monthly — exports an Excel file with all orders and bookings for the selected month
  • Download Full Report — exports everything (all orders, bookings, customers, products) into a 5-sheet Excel file
💡 Tip

Use the Monthly Report at the end of each month to keep a record of that month's sales. The Full Report is great for an annual review or sharing with an accountant.

Section 02
Orders
Manage all shop purchases

Every time a customer buys a product from your shop, it appears here as an Order. This section lets you track the order, update its status, and communicate with the customer.

📋 Order Status Types

Pending Confirmed Shipped Delivered Cancelled

When a new order comes in it starts as Pending. You move it through the stages as you process it.

📦 How to Process an Order — Step by Step

  1. Go to Orders in the left menu. You will see a list of all orders.
  2. Click on any order to open it. You will see the customer's name, address, items ordered, and total amount.
  3. Scroll down to "Update Status". Change the status (e.g. from Pending → Confirmed).
  4. Optionally add an Admin Note (e.g. "Dispatched via Blue Dart, tracking #123").
  5. Click Save. The customer will automatically receive an email with the update.
💡 Gift Orders

If an order is a gift, you will see a pink "GIFT ORDER" card on the order detail page. It shows the gift message the customer wrote. Write that message on a card and include it in the package before shipping.

💳 Payment Status

Unpaid Paid Failed

If a customer paid online but the payment did not update automatically, you can manually click "Mark as Paid" on the order page.

🔎 Filtering Orders

Use the filter dropdowns at the top of the Orders page to find orders by status (e.g. show only "Pending" orders) or search by customer name or order number.

Example

Customer calls to ask about their order. Search for their name or order number (like KMC-XXXXX) in the search box to find it instantly.

Section 03
Bookings
Service appointments and session management

When customers book a healing session or crystal service from your website, it appears here. You can approve, reject, or update the booking and the customer gets an email for every status change.

📋 Booking Status Types

Pending Approved Booked In Progress Completed Rejected Cancelled

✅ How to Approve a Booking

  1. Go to Bookings in the left menu.
  2. Click on a booking with Pending status.
  3. Review the customer's name, chosen service, date, and time slot.
  4. Change the status to Approved.
  5. Optionally add a note (e.g. "Please join 5 minutes early").
  6. Click Save — customer gets an email immediately.
💡 Tip — Use Status to Guide the Customer

Move the booking to In Progress when the session starts, and Completed when it's done. Each change sends the customer a friendly email so they always know what's happening.

⚠️ Important

If you cannot take a booking, change status to Rejected with a short reason in the note. The customer will be notified and can rebook at a different time.

Section 04
Products
Add, edit, price and manage your crystal products

This is where you manage everything in your shop. You can add new products, change prices, update stock, hide products, and delete old ones.

➕ Adding a New Product

  1. Click the "+ New Product" button (top right).
  2. Fill in the product name, category, description, and price.
  3. Upload a product image.
  4. Set the stock quantity (how many you have available).
  5. Make sure the "Visible on storefront" checkbox is ticked.
  6. Click Save. The product appears live on your shop immediately.

✏️ Quick Editing from the List

On the products list page, you can edit 3 things directly without opening the product:

  • Final Price (₹) — the amber input box. Type a new price and click Save.
  • USD Price ($) — the blue input box. Type a new USD price and click Save.
  • Stock — the stock number column. Click to change quantity and save.
Example

You want to run a sale on Rose Quartz Pendant. Find it in the list, change the Final Price from ₹600 to ₹499, and click Save. The new price shows instantly on the website.

👁️ Hiding / Showing a Product

  1. Click Edit on the product row.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the edit form.
  3. Find the checkbox: "Visible on the storefront (active status)".
  4. Untick it to hide the product, tick it to make it live again.
  5. Click Save.

🗑️ Trash / Deleted Products

When you delete a product it goes to Trash (not permanently deleted). Click the "Trash" button (top right with the count badge) to see deleted products and restore them if needed.

🔍 Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar at the top to find products by name, slug, or subcategory. Use the category dropdown to filter by crystal type. Use the Status dropdown to show only Live or Hidden products.

Section 05
Services
Crystal healing and spiritual services customers can book

Services are the healing sessions, consultations, and crystal programs that customers can book from the Services page on your website. Each service you create here becomes bookable online.

➕ How to Add a Service

  1. Click "+ New Service".
  2. Enter the service name (e.g. "Crystal Healing Session"), description, price, and duration.
  3. Upload a service image.
  4. Set available time slots or session details.
  5. Save — it appears on the Services page immediately.
💡 Tip

Keep the service description short and clear. Customers decide whether to book based on what they read here. Mention the duration and what they will experience.

Section 06
Home Services
Services offered at the customer's location

Home Services are similar to regular Services but specifically for sessions you offer at the customer's home (or a location of their choice). These are shown on the Home Services section of the website.

Managing them works the same way as Services — add, edit, and delete using the same steps as Section 05.

💡 Tip

Mention travel area or distance limit in the description so customers know if you can reach their location before they book.

Section 07
Blogs
Write and publish articles for your website

The Blog section lets you write and publish articles that appear on your website's Blog page. Good blog posts help with Google search rankings and keep your audience engaged.

✍️ How to Write a Blog Post

  1. Click "+ New Blog".
  2. Enter a title (e.g. "5 Crystals for Anxiety Relief").
  3. Write your content in the editor. You can add headings, bold text, and images.
  4. Add a cover image for the blog card.
  5. Set the status to Published when you're ready for it to go live.
  6. Save. The post appears on the Blog page immediately.
💡 Tip

Save as Draft first if you're not done writing. You can come back and finish it later. Only change to Published when the post is complete and ready.

Section 08
Testimonials
Customer reviews shown on your homepage

Testimonials are the customer quotes and feedback displayed on your homepage. You can add new ones manually, delete ones you don't want shown, and restore deleted ones from the Trash.

➕ Adding a Testimonial

  1. Click "+ Add Testimonial".
  2. Enter the customer's name, their message/quote, and their star rating.
  3. Optionally add their photo.
  4. Save — it appears on the homepage testimonial section.

🗑️ Deleting a Testimonial (Soft Delete)

When you click Delete on a testimonial, it moves to the Trash — it is not permanently gone. To see trashed testimonials, click the "Trash" tab at the top of the page.

  • Restore — brings it back to the live list
  • Delete Forever — permanently removes it (cannot be undone)
⚠️ Warning

"Delete Forever" is permanent. Only use it if you are 100% sure you don't need the testimonial again.

Section 09
Reviews
Product reviews left by customers on product pages

When customers buy products and leave reviews on the product page, they appear here. You control which reviews are visible on the website.

👁️ Approving / Hiding Reviews

Every review has a toggle button:

● Visible ○ Hidden
  • Visible (green) — the review is shown on the product page. Customers can read it.
  • Hidden (grey) — the review is hidden from the website. Only you can see it here.

Click the button to toggle between Visible and Hidden instantly.

💡 Best Practice

Hide reviews that contain inappropriate content or spam. Only delete reviews that are clearly fake. Keep genuine negative reviews visible — they build trust with customers.

Section 10
Gifting
Manage gift recipient categories shown on the Gifting page

Your website has a special Gifting Experience page where customers can browse crystals by who they want to gift them to (e.g. "For Mum", "For Best Friend", "For Partner"). This section lets you manage those recipient categories.

How the Gift Flow Works for Customers

  1. Customer visits the Gifting page on your website.
  2. They pick a recipient (e.g. "For Mum").
  3. They see curated products and can add a personal gift message.
  4. At checkout, the gift message is saved with the order.
  5. You see the message in the admin order detail — write it on a gift card before shipping.

📌 Gift Orders in Admin

When an order is a gift, the order detail page shows a pink "GIFT ORDER" card with the customer's message. This message must be handwritten on a gift card and included in the package.

💡 Tip

Always check the order detail for the GIFT ORDER card before packing. Never miss a gift message — it's why the customer chose your store for their special occasion.

Section 11
Reels
Short videos shown on your homepage

Reels are short videos (like Instagram Reels) that appear on your website's homepage. They help engage visitors and show your products and personality.

➕ Adding a New Reel

  1. Click "+ Add Reel".
  2. Enter a title for the reel.
  3. Upload your video file. A progress bar shows the upload status.
  4. Once uploaded, save. The reel appears on the homepage.

↕️ Changing the Order of Reels

You can drag and drop reels to reorder them. The order here is the order they appear on the homepage.

  1. Hover over any reel row — you will see a drag handle icon on the left.
  2. Click and drag the row up or down to the position you want.
  3. Release — the new order saves automatically.
💡 Tip

Put your best, most engaging reel at the top (position 1). Short videos under 30 seconds perform best.

Section 12
Messages
Customer enquiries from the Contact page

When a visitor fills in the Contact form on your website, their message arrives here. You can read all messages, reply to them, and keep track of customer enquiries.

📬 Reading a Message

  1. Click on any message in the list to open it.
  2. You will see the customer's name, email, and their full message.
  3. To reply, copy their email address and send them a reply from your Gmail.
💡 Tip

Try to reply to messages within 24 hours. Fast responses build customer trust and often turn enquiries into orders.

Section 13
Settings
Site-wide configuration for banners, carousel and more

Settings control global things on your website — like the homepage announcement banner, carousel images, and other site-wide options.

🖼️ Updating the Homepage Carousel

  1. Go to Settings.
  2. Find the Carousel Images section.
  3. Add, remove or reorder the images that rotate on your homepage banner.
  4. Save — changes go live immediately.

📢 Announcement Banner

You can set a site-wide announcement that appears at the top of every page (e.g. "Free shipping on orders above ₹499!" or "Sale on this weekend!").

⚠️ Caution

Be careful in Settings — changes here affect the entire website for all visitors. Always double-check before saving.

Section 14
Users
All registered customers on your website

Users are all the people who have created accounts on your website. This section is for viewing customer details. You don't need to do much here regularly.

🔎 Searching for a Customer

  1. Type the customer's name, email, or phone number in the search box.
  2. Click Search.
  3. Click on a customer to view their profile and order history.

When Would You Use This?

  • A customer says they can't log in — you can check if their account exists here.
  • You want to see a specific customer's purchase history.
  • Customer asks for their registered email — look it up here.
💡 Tip

The Users page shows total registered customers. Watching this number grow over time is a good indicator of your brand's popularity!

Quick Reference
Daily Admin Checklist
What to check every day

🌅 Morning (5 mins)

  1. Check Dashboard — any new orders or bookings overnight?
  2. Go to Orders → filter by Pending. Process any new orders.
  3. Go to Bookings → filter by Pending. Approve or respond to bookings.
  4. Check Messages — any new customer enquiries?

📦 Before Shipping

  1. Open the order in Orders.
  2. Check for the pink GIFT ORDER card — if present, write the message on a gift card.
  3. Update status to Shipped and add tracking number in the note.
  4. Customer gets an automatic email with the update.

📊 End of Month

  1. Go to Dashboard.
  2. Select the month from the filter.
  3. Click Download Monthly Report to save the Excel file.
  4. Review revenue and compare with last month.

🛒 When Stock Runs Low

  1. Go to Products.
  2. Find the product and update its Stock number inline.
  3. If fully out of stock, either set stock to 0 or Hide the product.
  4. When restocked, update stock and make it Live again.
